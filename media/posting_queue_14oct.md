Imagine a notebook shared by everyone in your apartment building. 📓🏢

Today most records live with one keeper: a bank, a registry, a company. If that book is wrong, lost or quietly changed, you just have to trust them.

A blockchain is a shared notebook that thousands of computers keep at once. Entries are grouped into pages (blocks), and each page carries a fingerprint of the one before it. Change an old entry and every page after it breaks, and every other copy disagrees. 🔗

- FB title: Omnix Explains: Your crypto isn't in your wallet
- YT title: Crypto wallets explained: your coins aren't in your wallet #Shorts
- YT category: Education · tags: crypto wallet, private key, seed phrase, recovery phrase, Omnix Explains, Omnix
- caption:
```
Your crypto isn't inside your wallet. 👛❌

A crypto wallet is more like a keyring. The coins live on the blockchain; the wallet holds the keys that prove they're yours. 🔑

Your public address is like your UPI ID: share it and people can pay you. Your private key is like your PIN and signature combined: it approves every payment. Most wallets turn it into 12 or 24 simple words, your recovery phrase. Guessing 24 words means searching a 78-digit number. No computer today comes close.

The catch: there's no "forgot password". Lose the words and the funds are gone. Share them and so are the funds. Never type them into a website, never photograph them, never give them to anyone. Real support will never ask. 🛡️

Whoever holds the keys holds the coins.

- video: `explains/explains4_stablecoins.mp4` · cover: `explains/explains4_stablecoins_thumb.jpg`
- FB title: Omnix Explains: What is a stablecoin?
- YT title: Stablecoins explained in 60 seconds: money home in minutes #Shorts
- YT category: Education · tags: stablecoin explained, what is a stablecoin, USDC, crypto basics, Omnix Explains, Omnix
- caption:
```
Imagine sending money to family abroad, and it arriving in minutes, on a Sunday night. 🌙💸

Crypto prices swing wildly; nobody wants their rent money to drop 10% overnight. A stablecoin is a crypto token designed to stay worth one fixed amount, usually one US dollar. 💵

Most are backed by reserves: for every token, the issuer says it holds a dollar in cash or short-term government bonds. Because they move on blockchains, they can cross borders at any hour and settle in minutes. Today more than $300 billion worth are in circulation. 🌍

The catch: a stablecoin is only as good as its reserves and its issuer. Some have lost their peg, and the rules differ from country to country. ⚠️

A stablecoin isn't an investment that grows. It's a digital dollar that moves fast.
